# Smart-Energy-Controller
A modular STM32-based smart energy monitoring and load control system with wireless connectivity, real-time power measurement, and remote switching.

## Overview

The Smart Energy Controller enables real-time monitoring of electrical consumption and wireless control of connected loads. The system is designed to support intelligent energy management through distributed monitoring, remote switching and centralised control.

- **Energy Monitoring:** Measurement of voltage, current, power, power factor and energy consumption.
- **Remote Switching:** Wireless control of connected electrical loads.
- **Real-Time Data:** Continuous transmission of electrical measurements.
- **Energy Management:** Automated load scheduling and power consumption optimisation.
- **Scalability:** Multiple controllers connected to a central monitoring system.
- **Remote Access:** Monitoring and control through a mobile device.
